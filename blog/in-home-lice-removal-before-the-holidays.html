Lice Before the Holidays? Skip the Clinic. We'll Come to You.

By Nicole Johnson  ·  November 2026  ·  4 min read

It's the week before Thanksgiving. The suitcases are half packed, the grandparents are expecting you, and then the school sends that email. Or you see your youngest scratching at the dinner table. Lice.

That's about the worst timing there is. But you don't have to pile everyone into the car, sit in a waiting room, or cancel your plans. LCA offers in-home lice removal across the Orlando area and in South Charlotte and Lake Norman. A certified technician comes to your house, checks the whole family, and treats anyone who needs it, right there in your kitchen.

Why the holidays are lice season

Lice spread through direct head-to-head contact. The holidays are basically one long stretch of that: cousins piled on the couch for movies, kids sharing beds at Grandma's, selfies with everyone squeezed into the frame, and long car rides side by side.

Add the fact that lice often spread at school in the weeks before break, and late November through early January ends up being one of the busiest times of year for lice calls.

💡 The best time to get treated is before you travel. Bringing lice to a family gathering means you may come home to a second round, plus a few awkward phone calls to relatives.
